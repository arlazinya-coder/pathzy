# South Africa Employment Adapter Specification

Status: Specification only
Date: 2026-08-04
Code: `lib/employment-intelligence/domain/country-context.ts`

Phase 3A does not add live South African facts. It defines the future adapter contract.

The South Africa adapter must support sourced contracts for:

- provinces
- major employment hubs
- NQF
- SAQA and foreign qualification recognition
- matric and non-matric pathways
- TVET
- SETA-related pathways
- learnerships
- apprenticeships
- internships
- graduate programmes
- public employment services
- youth employment programmes
- work-authorisation categories
- recruitment conventions
- CV expectations
- salary data
- multilingual context
- rural, township, and urban access
- formal and informal pathways

Every external fact requires source metadata, retrieved or effective date, version, confidence, and expiry or update policy.
