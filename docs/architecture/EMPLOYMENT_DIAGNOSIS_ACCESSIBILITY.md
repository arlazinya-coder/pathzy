# Employment Diagnosis Accessibility

Phase 3D supports four presentation modes:

- `STANDARD`
- `PLAIN_LANGUAGE`
- `HIGH_GUIDANCE`
- `ASSISTED`

The live interface renders one question per screen, structured options, visible progress, keyboard-usable buttons, opt-out choices for sensitive questions, and concise help text.

Limited digital access and lower reading/writing comfort change presentation and support. They do not lower capability or human worth.
