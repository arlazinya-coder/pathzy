# Employment Intelligence AI Policy

Status: Phase 3A policy
Code: `lib/employment-intelligence/domain/index.ts`

## AI may later support

- summarising open-text answers
- identifying possible transferable skills
- generating supportive explanations
- adapting plain-language wording
- proposing career directions
- drafting Career Plan wording

## AI must not independently determine

- identity completion
- work eligibility
- evidence verification
- hard eligibility
- canonical readiness bands without validated structured rules
- route state
- ownership
- legal conclusions

No AI calls are implemented in Phase 3A.
