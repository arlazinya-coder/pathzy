# Employment Intelligence Fairness

Status: Phase 3A principles

1. Do not penalise users for lacking privilege.
2. Separate user-controlled barriers from external barriers.
3. Do not infer protected attributes.
4. Do not use sensitive attributes to reduce opportunity unfairly.
5. Disability information may only support accessibility or lawful accommodation.
6. Nationality may only inform work-authorisation and country context.
7. Long unemployment requires support, not stigma.
8. Informal experience counts as potential evidence.
9. No degree does not equal low capability.
10. Low literacy changes presentation and support, not human value.
11. All recommendations must remain explainable.
12. Users may correct or reject intelligence conclusions.
13. PATHZY must not make employer hiring decisions.
14. No automatic job application occurs without explicit approval.
