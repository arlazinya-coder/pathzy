# Employment Intelligence Fixture Corpus

Status: Phase 3B implemented.

The fixture corpus lives in `lib/employment-intelligence/engine/fixtures.ts`.

## Covered Segments

- empty profile
- graduate with no formal experience
- technical project profile
- cleaner or facilities support with informal experience
- security worker
- domestic worker
- retail worker
- driver
- administrator
- technician
- career changer
- return-to-work user
- limited internet access
- transport constraint
- low literacy or communication support
- immediate income need
- French-language candidate
- senior executive

## Fixture Rules

- Fixtures are fictional.
- Fixtures do not encode one candidate, employer, role, or profession.
- Fixtures test cross-segment behaviour.
- Fixtures are deterministic and local.
- Fixtures do not require database, network, or AI providers.
