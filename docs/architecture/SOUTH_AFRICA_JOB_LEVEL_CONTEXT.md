# South Africa Job-Level Context

Implemented in:

`lib/employment-intelligence/country/adapters/south-africa/job-level-mapping.ts`

The adapter maps internal Phase 3A job levels to cautious South Africa context:

- FOUNDATIONAL
- SERVICE
- OPERATIONAL
- CLERICAL
- TECHNICAL
- SKILLED_TRADE
- GRADUATE
- PROFESSIONAL
- MANAGEMENT
- EXECUTIVE
- ENTREPRENEURIAL

Examples are for internal testing only. No current salary, market demand, or role availability claims are included.

Security, service, technical, trade and professional dependency contexts are represented as evidence requirements rather than eligibility conclusions.
