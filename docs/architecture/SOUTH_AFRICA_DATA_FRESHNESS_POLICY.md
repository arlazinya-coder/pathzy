# South Africa Data Freshness Policy

Freshness states:

- CURRENT
- REVIEW_DUE
- STALE
- UNAVAILABLE
- UNKNOWN

Rules:

- Current-sensitive facts require source metadata.
- Stale facts lower confidence.
- Unavailable data cannot be presented as confirmed.
- Salary values require source, effective date, sample information and confidence.
- Programme eligibility and availability require verified programme data.
- Market-demand claims require verified labour-market data.

The deterministic engine does not fetch live data.
