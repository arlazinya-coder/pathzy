# Personalised Home Intelligence

The authenticated Home remains focused on four major areas:

1. Freshness and current intelligence state.
2. Primary Next-Best-Action.
3. Employment position summary.
4. Career Plan and secondary tools.

Home shows one primary action and at most three secondary actions. It uses persisted intelligence and does not calculate readiness locally.

Empty states route users to Employment Diagnosis or Professional Identity rather than inventing readiness.
