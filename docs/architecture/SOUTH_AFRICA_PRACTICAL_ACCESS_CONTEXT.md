# South Africa Practical Access Context

Implemented in:

`lib/employment-intelligence/country/adapters/south-africa/practical-access-context.ts`

The adapter models:

- smartphone-only access
- shared device
- computer access
- stable or limited internet
- email and document-upload ability
- online application ability
- digital confidence
- local travel radius
- public or own transport
- shift travel constraints
- relocation willingness
- remote-work preference
- province/city mismatch
- user-reported safety concern
- low-literacy support
- immediate-income urgency

Rules:

- Limited access affects Digital and Practical Access only.
- It does not reduce Skills, Qualification, or human potential.
- PATHZY does not infer poverty, safety, commute cost, or travel time.
